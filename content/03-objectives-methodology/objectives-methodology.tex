\section{Objectives}

The goal of this study is to create an online course for teaching the \ac{uml} class diagram and analyze various approaches to doing so. The target audience are computer science students taking a software modeling course at \ac{fiit}. %The success of the proposed online course will be decided by tests and questionnaires.

It is a project that aims to compare 2 of the most prominent approaches to enhancing engagement and information retention in e-learning - competition and play - and applying them to teaching software modeling.

In such a case, other researchers could extend the course by adding additional features and content, including other types of diagrams in it, or by adjusting the materials to try out different e-learning approaches to delivering content and driving engagement. On top of that, e-learning materials in other areas might be developed using findings in this project.

\section{Methodology}

The courses will be implemented in an established e-learning platform. They will contain theoretical materials, interactive exercises, self-assessment components, as well as e-learning practices based on our analysis. Both courses will contain the same exercises and materials, but they will differ in tone and use of e-learning practices. That will allow for separate analysis of the educational approaches.

The courses will be taken by second-year students of computer science who will be presented with 2 tests and 2 questionnaires - one of each at the start, and one of each at the end of the courses. They will be used to measure the students' interest and level of knowledge before and after taking the course. Each student will only take 1 of the courses.

The tests will be made up of theoretical questions and practical exercises, while the questionnaires will be aimed at the students' subjective reception and interest. The most important questions will target the students' interest in such materials and whether they think it is an improvement over the standard way of teaching software modeling.
